Notes

Sheet notes

  1. 1

    PX625 is a mercuric-oxide cell: 1.35 V in the same Ø11.6 × 5.4 mm shell. Mercuric-oxide button cells were withdrawn from the United States market after 1996. A meter calibrated around 1.35 V reads high on any 1.5 V replacement. The shell fits; the scale does not.

  2. 2

    LR43 and SR43 hold the same Ø11.6 mm but stand 1.2 mm shorter. They drop into the well and may never reach the lid contact. A spacer is a repair, not a substitution.

  3. 3

    CR2032 is drawn here only to be dismissed. Ø20.0 × 3.2 mm at 3.00 V shares nothing with this shell, and the break line says so before the caption does.

  4. 4

    The two questions are independent. LR43 sits exactly on the voltage datum and still cannot be fitted; SR44 fits perfectly and still moves the reading. That is why the millimeters and the volts are dimensioned on the same sheet and never merged into one score.

  5. 5

    Ad nodes. The three slots on this sheet are written into the page only when a creative is served. Nothing is emitted as a placeholder, so no empty box ever ships. Every gap on the sheet comes from a grid gap or a rule on the parent, never from a rule that counts children, so a slot can appear or vanish without moving anything else by one pixel.
