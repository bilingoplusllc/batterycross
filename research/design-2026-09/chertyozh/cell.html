Method

What this sheet claims

Dimensions are published nominal figures for the shell, not measurements of an individual cell. Voltage is nominal, not voltage under load. “Same shell” means the diameter and height match to a tenth of a millimeter as published; it does not promise that the terminal geometry or the contact pressure in your device will accept the cell.

Where the file carries no figure, the sheet prints a dash and draws nothing. There are no photographs on this site and no estimated numbers: if a line is on the drawing, a figure in the file put it there.

Notes

Sheet notes

  1. 1

    The cap, crimp and gasket in Detail A are drafting conventions, not data. Only the two lettered dimensions come from the file: Ø11.6 and 5.4. Everything the file does not know is drawn as a convention or not drawn at all.

  2. 2

    0.05 V is inside the spread a single cell moves through as it runs down, which is why SR44 sits inside the hatched band. 0.15 V is not: it shifts every reading by the same amount, in the same direction, forever. That is the whole difference between View B’s two dimension lines.

  3. 3

    PX625 has no exact replacement. SR44 fills the identical well and reads 0.20 V high against a 1.35 V scale; the crossed arrow in View C is the sheet refusing to call that a substitution.

  4. 4

    Ad nodes. The unit in the side column is written into the page only when a creative is served. No placeholder is emitted, so no empty box ships, and no spacing rule on this sheet counts its children.
